# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own quality strategy, test planning, and acceptance validation. They collaborate with Developers, Product Managers, and Project Managers to ensure features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards before release.

### Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain test plans and test cases for features
- Coordinate manual and automated testing across environments
- Validate acceptance criteria are met before PR approval
- Identify and document defects with clear reproduction steps
- Recommend testing strategy for high-risk or complex features
- Support release verification and smoke testing

### Goals
- Ensure high-quality releases with minimal production defects
- Reduce cycle time for QA without sacrificing quality
- Build team confidence in test coverage and validation

### Typical Communication
- QA sign-off in PR reviews with Developers
- Test results and quality metrics in sprint retrospectives with Project Managers
- Risk escalation for untested or high-complexity features to Product Managers and Project Managers

---

## Technical Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide technical governance, design oversight, and architectural direction. They own technical strategy and help Developers, Product Managers, and Project Managers navigate complex technical decisions and risks.

### Responsibilities
- Review technical designs and architecture for scalability and maintainability
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations during planning with Project Managers
- Mentor Developers and ensure code quality standards are met
- Make technical trade-off decisions in collaboration with Product Managers and Project Managers
- Support dependency management and cross-team technical integration

### Goals
- Maintain technical excellence and system health
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Facilitate knowledge sharing and Developer growth

### Typical Communication
- Design review comments and technical guidance for Developers
- Technical risk escalation and mitigation planning with Project Managers
- Architecture documentation and decision logs shared with Product Managers

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, decision authority, and resource commitments. They ensure projects align with organizational strategy and priorities, and partner with Product Managers and Project Managers to remove blockers and approve direction.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project charter and business case in collaboration with Product Managers
- Make prioritization decisions when trade-offs arise, in partnership with Product Managers
- Remove organizational blockers and secure resources for Project Managers
- Receive regular status updates and escalations from Project Managers
- Champion project outcomes and communicate value to the broader organization

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver business value and ROI
- Maintain alignment with organizational strategy
- Remove barriers to project success

### Typical Communication
- Monthly executive status updates from Project Managers
- Decision escalations and approval requests from Product Managers
- Risk and blocker escalation from Project Managers

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate deployment activities, release communication, and post-release verification. They ensure releases are executed smoothly and that Developers, Project Managers, and stakeholders are aligned and informed throughout the release lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release schedules and deployment windows with Project Managers and Developers
- Prepare and communicate release notes to stakeholders and support teams
- Execute or oversee deployment to staging and production with Developers
- Run post-release smoke tests and verification, coordinating with QA/Testing Leads
- Coordinate incident response if deployment issues arise
- Maintain release documentation and rollback procedures

### Goals
- Deliver releases with minimal risk and downtime
- Ensure smooth stakeholder communication during releases
- Maintain clear deployment and rollback procedures

### Typical Communication
- Release schedules and deployment windows shared with Project Managers and Developers
- Release notes and change summaries distributed to stakeholders
- Incident notifications and status updates coordinated with Developers and Project Managers

---

## UX/Design Lead

### Role Summary
UX/Design Leads ensure solutions prioritize user needs, usability, and accessibility. They collaborate with Product Managers on requirements, Developers on implementation, and Project Managers on timelines to deliver intuitive experiences throughout the design and delivery lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and validation to inform design decisions in partnership with Product Managers
- Create and iterate on design prototypes and specifications for Developers
- Review feature designs for usability and accessibility
- Collaborate with Developers on implementation of design intent
- Validate features meet usability goals post-release, working alongside QA/Testing Leads

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible, and delightful user experiences
- Reduce user friction and support burden through better design
- Maintain consistency in design language and patterns

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and feedback shared with Developers and Product Managers
- User research insights and usability findings presented to Product Managers and Project Managers
- Design specifications and interaction documentation provided to Developers
